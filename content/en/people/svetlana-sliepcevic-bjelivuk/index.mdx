My research interests lie at the intersection of lexicology, lexicography, phraseology, cognitive linguistics and discourse analysis, with a particular focus on Serbian and its relations with other Slavic and non-Slavic languages. I am especially interested in the ways in which meaning is structured, conceptualized and represented across languages, cultures and communicative contexts. My current research focuses on the development of a conceptual bilingual phraseological dictionary for the Serbian–German language pair.
